How to install

  1. Tap Download for AndroidWait for the download to finish. Use Wi-Fi if you can.
  2. Open the fileTap Open on the download message, or find mix-and-blooms.apk in your Downloads.
  3. Allow the installIf your phone blocks it, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, then go back.
  4. Install and openTap Install. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap More details → Install anyway.

Having trouble?

It says "App not installed"

Uninstall the Mix & Blooms app that's already on your phone, then install again.

The download stopped

Check your internet connection and tap Download again.

How do I update the app?

Open this page again and tap Download. Installing the new file updates your app.
